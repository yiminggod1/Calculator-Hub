How Savings Calculator works

Estimate how savings can grow with interest and regular deposits over time. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

Savings growth applies the periodic interest rate to the starting balance and recurring deposits over the selected number of periods.

Worked example

A $1,000 starting balance with $100 monthly deposits can be projected over five years using the selected annual rate.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Starting savings ($), Annual interest rate (%), Years, Compounds per year, Deposit per period ($). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.

Frequently asked question

Does this savings calculator include every real-world cost?

No. The calculator focuses on the variables shown in its fields; taxes, fees, insurance, closing costs, or other charges may need separate treatment.
