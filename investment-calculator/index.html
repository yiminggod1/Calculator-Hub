How Investment Calculator works

Estimate the future value of an investment with an initial amount, expected return, time period, and recurring contributions. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

Future value combines the starting balance and recurring contributions with the selected periodic return and number of periods.

Worked example

A $5,000 starting balance plus $200 monthly contributions can be projected forward using the chosen annual return and time horizon.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Initial investment ($), Expected annual return (%), Years invested, Compounds per year, Contribution per period ($). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.

Frequently asked question

Does this investment calculator include every real-world cost?

No. The calculator focuses on the variables shown in its fields; taxes, fees, insurance, closing costs, or other charges may need separate treatment.
