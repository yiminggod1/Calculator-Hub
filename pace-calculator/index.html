Calculation notes

How Pace Calculator works

A free, fast pace calculator with a clear result and helpful explanation. This section explains the underlying rule and gives a concrete example for checking the output.

Formula or rule

Running pace = elapsed time ÷ distance; speed is the inverse relationship, distance ÷ time.

Worked example

A 5-mile run in 45 minutes has a pace of 9 minutes per mile.

What the inputs mean

The calculator uses Time (minutes), Distance (miles or km). Enter the values exactly as the field labels specify; for dates and times, pay particular attention to the intended day, zone, and endpoint.

How to interpret the result

Use this as a general estimate and keep the measurement method and units consistent.
