Calculation notes

How Time Zone Converter works

Convert a local date and time between two time zones using your browser's time zone database. This section explains the underlying rule and gives a concrete example for checking the output.

Formula or rule

A time-zone conversion represents the same instant in another zone, applying the source and destination UTC offsets including daylight-saving rules where available.

Worked example

9:00 AM in New York and 9:00 AM in Los Angeles are different instants; the converter shifts the clock time to preserve the same instant.

What the inputs mean

The calculator uses Date, Time, From time zone, To time zone. Enter the values exactly as the field labels specify; for dates and times, pay particular attention to the intended day, zone, and endpoint.

How to interpret the result

Check whether your use case is inclusive or exclusive and confirm the date/time convention before comparing results.

Frequently asked question

Why can two date or time calculations differ by one day or an hour?

The difference often comes from endpoint conventions, midnight crossing, time-zone offsets, or daylight-saving changes. Check the assumptions shown on the page.
