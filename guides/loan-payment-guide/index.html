How to Calculate a Loan Payment

A practical explanation of principal, APR, term, monthly payment, and total interest.

ADVERTISEMENT

The standard payment formula

For a fixed-rate installment loan, the monthly payment can be calculated from the principal, monthly interest rate, and number of monthly payments. If the monthly rate is r and the number of payments is n, the payment is P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n−1).

Example

Suppose a loan has a $20,000 principal, a 6% annual rate, and a 5-year term. The monthly rate is 0.06 ÷ 12 and the term is 60 payments. The calculator applies the formula and also estimates total interest over the full schedule.

What changes the payment?

  • A larger principal increases the payment.
  • A higher interest rate increases the payment.
  • A longer term usually lowers the monthly payment but can increase total interest.
