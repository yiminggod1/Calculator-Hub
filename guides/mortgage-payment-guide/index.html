How Mortgage Payments Are Calculated

A standard fixed-rate mortgage payment depends on the loan principal, monthly interest rate, and number of monthly payments.

Formula

For a monthly rate r and n monthly payments, payment = P × r(1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1).

What is not included?

Property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, points, and other costs may change the amount actually paid each month.
