How to Calculate Tips and Discounts

Two everyday percentage calculations, explained with formulas and examples.

ADVERTISEMENT

Tip formula

Tip = bill × tip rate ÷ 100. Total = bill + tip. If a bill is $80 and the tip rate is 20%, the tip is $16 and the total is $96.

Splitting a bill

After adding the tip, divide the total by the number of people. The calculator uses at least one person so an empty or invalid party-size field does not produce a divide-by-zero result.

Discount formula

Discount amount = original price × discount rate ÷ 100. Sale price = original price − discount amount. For example, 25% off $120 saves $30, leaving a $90 sale price.
