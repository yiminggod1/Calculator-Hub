How Unit Conversion Works

Understand conversion factors and common metric-to-imperial conversions.

ADVERTISEMENT

Use a common base unit

A reliable conversion method is to convert the source quantity into a shared base unit, then convert that base quantity into the target unit. This prevents mixing incompatible dimensions.

Examples

One kilometer is 1,000 meters. One mile is 1,609.344 meters. One kilogram is 2.2046226218 pounds. One U.S. gallon is 3.785411784 liters.

Temperature is different

Length, mass, and volume use multiplicative conversion factors. Temperature conversions also include an offset, so Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin require different formulas.
