How Retirement Savings Growth Is Estimated

A transparent look at the inputs behind a basic future-value projection.

ADVERTISEMENT

Five important inputs

Starting balance, annual return, time, compounding frequency, and recurring contribution all affect a future-value estimate. Increasing the time horizon can have a large effect because growth compounds on earlier growth.

Contributions

Calculator Hub models recurring contributions as being added at the end of each compounding period. Real investment accounts may use different deposit timing, fees, taxes, or changing returns.

Why projections are not guarantees

A fixed annual return is a mathematical assumption, not a forecast. Investment performance can vary from year to year and losses are possible.
