Why payment size matters

Interest is charged on the outstanding balance. When the payment is only slightly above the interest charged for the month, the balance declines slowly. A larger payment generally reduces principal faster.

Inputs used by the calculator

The Calculator Hub debt tool uses your current balance, annual percentage rate, and monthly payment. It estimates the number of months using a fixed-rate amortization model.

Important limitation

Real credit accounts can include fees, changing rates, minimum-payment rules, promotional rates, and new charges. Use the result as an estimate rather than a statement from a lender.
