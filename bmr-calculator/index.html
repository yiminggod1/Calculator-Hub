Calculation notes

How BMR Calculator works

A free, fast bmr calculator with a clear result and helpful explanation. The details here explain the calculation instead of leaving the result as a single unexplained number.

Formula and method

BMR estimates resting energy expenditure from age, sex, height, and weight using the selected formula.

Worked example

For the same weight and height, changing age or sex changes the formula inputs and therefore the estimated resting energy need.

What the inputs mean

The page uses Weight (kg), Height (cm), Age (years), Sex. Enter each value in the unit shown and keep related measurements consistent.

How to interpret the result

Use the number as a general estimate, not a diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendation. Measurement method, individual characteristics, and context can materially affect health-related estimates.
