Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be processed when you use Calculator Hub, including browser-based calculations, advertising, and optional analytics.

Information used by the calculators

Calculator inputs are designed to be processed in your browser for the calculations provided by the site. The site does not require an account to use the calculators. If a particular third-party service is enabled on the site, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to measure advertising performance and, where permitted, personalize ads. Users in regions with applicable consent requirements may be shown a consent message before certain advertising technologies are used.

Analytics and third-party services

Calculator Hub may use third-party services for hosting, security, measurement, or advertising. Those services may receive technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, or page activity according to their own policies and the settings enabled for the site.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change. The current version published on this page applies to future use of the site.

Frequently asked questions

Does using a calculator require an account?

No account is required for the calculator tools.

Will advertising use cookies?

If advertising is enabled, applicable advertising technologies may use cookies or similar technologies subject to consent requirements and provider policies.
