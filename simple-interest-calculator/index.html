How Simple Interest Calculator works

Use this free simple interest calculator to get a fast estimate in your browser. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

Simple interest is I = PRT, and final amount A = P + I.

Worked example

$2,000 at 5% for 3 years earns $300 of simple interest, for a total of $2,300.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Principal ($), Annual interest rate (%), Time (years). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.
