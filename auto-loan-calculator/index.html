How Auto Loan Calculator works

Estimate car loan payments, total interest, and total repayment from vehicle price, APR, and term. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

Auto-loan payment uses the financed amount after the down payment, then applies the fixed-payment amortization formula.

Worked example

A $35,000 vehicle with a $5,000 down payment finances $30,000 before taxes, fees, or trade-in adjustments.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Vehicle price ($), APR (%), Loan term (years), Down payment ($). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.

Frequently asked question

Does this auto loan calculator include every real-world cost?

No. The calculator focuses on the variables shown in its fields; taxes, fees, insurance, closing costs, or other charges may need separate treatment.
