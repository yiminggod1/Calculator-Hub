Calculation notes

How Paint Calculator works

Estimate gallons of paint needed from area, coverage, and coats. The calculation notes make the conversion or estimate transparent and easier to verify.

Formula or method

Paint area is estimated from surface dimensions, then divided by coverage per gallon and adjusted for the number of coats.

Worked example

A 400-square-foot paintable area at 350 sq ft per gallon needs about 1.15 gallons for one coat before rounding for purchase.
