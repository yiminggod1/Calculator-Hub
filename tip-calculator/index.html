Calculation notes

How Tip Calculator works

Calculate tip, total bill, and your final payment. No signup required. The details here explain the calculation instead of leaving the result as a single unexplained number.

Formula and method

Tip = bill × tip rate; total = bill + tip, and a per-person amount divides the total by the party size.

Worked example

On a $80 bill with a 20% tip for four people, the tip is $16 and the total is $96, or $24 each.

What the inputs mean

The page uses Bill amount ($), Tip percentage (%), Number of people. Enter each value in the unit shown and keep related measurements consistent.

How to interpret the result

Use the displayed assumptions as the scope of the result. Real-world costs, rates, fees, or timing can change the final outcome.
