Calculation notes

How Days Between Dates Calculator works

A free, fast days between dates calculator with a clear result and helpful explanation. This section explains the underlying rule and gives a concrete example for checking the output.

Formula or rule

Days between dates is the calendar-date difference after converting each date to a day count.

Worked example

January 1 to January 31 is 30 days apart in a non-leap year.

What the inputs mean

The calculator uses Start date, End date. Enter the values exactly as the field labels specify; for dates and times, pay particular attention to the intended day, zone, and endpoint.

How to interpret the result

Check whether your use case is inclusive or exclusive and confirm the date/time convention before comparing results.

Frequently asked question

Why can two date or time calculations differ by one day or an hour?

The difference often comes from endpoint conventions, midnight crossing, time-zone offsets, or daylight-saving changes. Check the assumptions shown on the page.
