How Mortgage Calculator works

Estimate monthly mortgage payments, total interest, and total cost from loan amount, rate, and term. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

Monthly payment M = P·r(1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n−1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments.

Worked example

A $300,000 balance at 6.5% for 30 years produces a principal-and-interest payment of about $1,896.20 per month.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Home price / loan amount, Annual interest rate (%), Loan term (years). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.

Frequently asked question

Does this mortgage calculator include every real-world cost?

No. The calculator focuses on the variables shown in its fields; taxes, fees, insurance, closing costs, or other charges may need separate treatment.
