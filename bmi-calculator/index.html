Calculation notes

How BMI Calculator works

Calculate body mass index from height and weight as a general adult screening measure. The details here explain the calculation instead of leaving the result as a single unexplained number.

Formula and method

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in meters².

Worked example

At 70 kg and 1.75 m, BMI = 70 ÷ 1.75² ≈ 22.9.

What the inputs mean

The page uses Weight (kg), Height (cm). Enter each value in the unit shown and keep related measurements consistent.

How to interpret the result

Use the number as a general estimate, not a diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendation. Measurement method, individual characteristics, and context can materially affect health-related estimates.
