How to use this calculator

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. BMI is a screening measure, not a direct measurement of body fat, and its interpretation can vary by age, sex, muscle mass, and other factors.

How is the bmi calculated?

BMI is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. BMI is a screening measure, not a direct measurement of body fat, and its interpretation can vary by age, sex, muscle mass, and other factors.
