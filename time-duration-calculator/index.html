How Time Duration Calculator works

Calculate the elapsed time between two clock times in hours and minutes. This section explains the underlying rule and gives a concrete example for checking the output.

Formula or rule

Elapsed duration is the difference between an end time and a start time, with midnight crossing handled as a next-day interval when applicable.

Worked example

23:30 to 01:15 is 1 hour 45 minutes when the end time is on the following day.

What the inputs mean

The calculator uses Start time, End time. Enter the values exactly as the field labels specify; for dates and times, pay particular attention to the intended day, zone, and endpoint.

How to interpret the result

Check whether your use case is inclusive or exclusive and confirm the date/time convention before comparing results.

Frequently asked question

Why can two date or time calculations differ by one day or an hour?

The difference often comes from endpoint conventions, midnight crossing, time-zone offsets, or daylight-saving changes. Check the assumptions shown on the page.
