How Retirement Calculator works

Project how current savings and regular contributions could grow before retirement using a hypothetical return. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

A retirement projection compares a starting balance plus recurring savings against an assumed periodic return over the accumulation period.

Worked example

Saving $500 monthly for 25 years at an assumed 6% annual return illustrates how both contributions and compounding affect the projected balance.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Current savings ($), Expected annual return (%), Years until retirement, Compounds per year, Contribution per period ($). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.

Frequently asked question

Does this retirement calculator include every real-world cost?

No. The calculator focuses on the variables shown in its fields; taxes, fees, insurance, closing costs, or other charges may need separate treatment.
