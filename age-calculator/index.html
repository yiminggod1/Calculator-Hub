How Age Calculator works

Calculate your current age from a date of birth with a clear browser-based result. This section explains the underlying rule and gives a concrete example for checking the output.

Formula or rule

Completed age is determined by comparing the birth date with the target date, accounting for whether the birthday has occurred in the target year.

Worked example

Someone born June 15, 1990 is 36 on June 15, 2026, but still 35 on June 14, 2026.

What the inputs mean

The calculator uses Date of birth. Enter the values exactly as the field labels specify; for dates and times, pay particular attention to the intended day, zone, and endpoint.

How to interpret the result

Frequently asked question
