How Compound Interest Calculator works

See how an initial balance and recurring contributions can grow with compound interest over time. The explanation below gives the mathematical model, a worked example, and practical checks so the result can be reproduced.

Formula

With periodic compounding, A = P(1+r/n)^(nt); regular end-of-period contributions add their future value separately.

Worked example

$10,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about $16,470 before any additional contributions.

What the inputs mean

This page asks for Starting balance ($), Annual rate (%), Years, Compounds per year, Contribution per period ($). Keep the units and time periods consistent; for rates, enter the percentage in the format shown by the field.

How to interpret the result

Treat the output as an estimate of the mathematical model. Real financing can differ because of taxes, fees, payment timing, rate changes, or lender terms.
