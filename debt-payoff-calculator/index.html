How Debt Payoff Calculator works

Estimate payoff time and interest for a balance with a fixed monthly payment. The details here explain the calculation instead of leaving the result as a single unexplained number.

Formula and method

For a fixed payment, each period reduces the balance by payment minus interest; interest for a period is balance × APR ÷ 12.

Worked example

A $5,000 balance at 18% APR with a $150 monthly payment is reduced by $75 of first-month interest before principal is applied.

What the inputs mean

The page uses Current balance ($), Annual interest rate (%), Monthly payment ($). Enter each value in the unit shown and keep related measurements consistent.

How to interpret the result
